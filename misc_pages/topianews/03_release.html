Release

The plan

The plan was to create a site where Bricktopia members can get a little more information about what Bricktopia is and some information about the Moderating Team, as well as a place where Lego news and reviews are posted.

The development

The development was done by me, Legocity2000, I'm a hobby-developer and creating the site was a great opportunity to practice coding. But sadly I'm not capable of doing mobile-optimalization, that is over my level.

The future plans

The Lego News section is planned to be handled not only by me or the Team, but by the community as well. If you want to write set reviews, collect the upcoming releases or write other Lego-related articles, then contact us! And more pages or features might come if the interest stays and we find something that is requested and I can do it.

Feedback

On Discord we opened a Feedback and a Bug Report channel, if you want to leave something there, just do it, I'm looking forward to see feedback, and bugs have to be fixed.

Acknowledgement

I have already made an Acknowledgement page, so here I keep it short: thanks to the people who helped me in any way in the last 1.5 months while I was creating this site, thanks to the HEATLabs team, who helped me when I got stuck and (last but not least!) thanks to BKendl, Haflan and SINEWAVE for testing and early bug reporting.
