Bricktopia Leadership Election

Lastly, I'd like to share an important announcement regarding Bricktopia's future.

Later this year, we'll be holding an election to select a new Server Director who will help lead Bricktopia's day to day operations.

The first nominated candidates are:

• @Legocity2000

• @Master Fisto

• @Lord darknightslayer100

However, applications are open to everyone.

If you believe you have what it takes to help lead this community, feel free to apply. Tell us why you'd like to take on the role, what your goals are, and how you would help Bricktopia continue to grow.

To avoid any confusion: I'm not leaving the server. I will remain the Founder and continue supporting Bricktopia. The elected successor will mainly oversee community activities, events, and the server's daily management, while I'll continue providing guidance, resources, and support behind the scenes.

I wish all candidates the best of luck, and I'm looking forward to seeing what ideas you have for Bricktopia's future! ❤️
